## Report (Claude, 2026-10-30)

### What changed

**`static/css/system.css`**

- **Section 1** gains the tokens from DESIGN_DIRECTION.md 5.2 to 5.4, 5.8 and 6.1:
  - `--fs-*`, `--lh-*`, `--fw-*`;
  - `--pad-*` and `--gap-*`;
  - `--r-round`;
  - the 15 `--layer-*` values;
  - 12 `--dur-*` and 6 `--ease-*` values;
  - `--motion-distance` and `--motion-scale-from`.

  Two media blocks are added: the reduced-motion block now also zeroes the motion distance, and a phone block (max-width 640px) steps `--fs-2xl` and `--fs-3xl` down one size. Nothing existing changed.
- **Section 1b holds the colour roles,** declared on `:root, body`.
  - **Why `body` too:** a custom property resolves `var()` where it is declared and is inherited already resolved. A `:root`-only `--c-page: var(--bg-page)` would therefore keep the PF2e value inside `body.system-cosmere`, whose theme re-declares the primitives on `<body>`. The plan's "add once in `:root`" would not have worked.
  - **Theme blocks:** `body.system-cosmere` and `body.system-cosmere.cosmere-mistborn` hold the cells that are not plain aliases (on-accent, info, focus, the HP tiers, the chart).
  - **Mood tokens:** each mood has three values (pill fill, pill border, vignette), so they are named `--c-mood-<name>`, `-line` and `-wash`.
- **27a.** Only the `:root` override is deleted. The `body` background rule stays, with a comment. 27b onward is untouched.
- **Cosmere pin, and a correction to the earlier handoff.**
  - The previous handoff said Cosmere needed `--border-strong` pinned. That is wrong. Section 2 (`system.css:374`) re-declares `--border-strong: var(--border-rule)` on `html, body`, so inside `<body>` it was always the theme's own value. Pinning 0.12 would have turned Stormlight and Mistborn hairlines white. A test caught it before any screenshot was taken.
  - What does need care is `<html>` itself. On a Cosmere page it paints with `:root` values:
    - its background (`html, body { background: var(--bg-page) }`), below short content;
    - its text colour;
    - the page scrollbar (`base.html`'s unscoped `::-webkit-scrollbar-thumb`).

    `html:has(> body.system-cosmere)` keeps those four values (`--bg-page`, `--border-rule`, `--text-on-dark`, `--text-on-dark-3`) as they were, with a comment. A Cosmere screen package can later switch them to the theme's own colours.

**New files**

- **`static/css/ds-tokens.css`** is generated by the new `tools/design_tokens.py`.
  - The tool models the cascade for `<html>` and `<body>` in each theme: specificity, `!important`, `:has()`, and `var()` resolved on the declaring element.
  - The file holds 128 custom properties with canonical PF2e literals (no `var()`), plus the two media blocks.
  - `python tools/design_tokens.py check` and `tests/test_ds_tokens.py` fail on drift.
- **`static/css/ds.css`** is empty apart from the cascade-contract comment. It is linked in nine templates.
- **`tools/check_contrast.py`** reuses the resolver.
  - **Enforced, exit 1 below the floor:** every text role on the surface; on-accent on accent; the status-badge text on its 22% tint (4.5:1); focus on surface and page (3:1).
  - **Reported only:** hairlines, status borders and HP fills (WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1 only where the edge is the sole cue).
- **`docs/design/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`** covers the six MIT sources with the copyright lines recorded in the plan, and says what each is used for. The transitions.dev line points at its upstream LICENSE rather than guessing a holder.
- **`tools/ui_screens.py`** is the before/after screenshot harness the previous handoff called the next step.
  - It reuses the `ui_inventory` fixtures (the PF2e party, or a fresh signed-in campaign per system), serves the app on a local port, and shoots each route in headless Chromium.
  - Shots are taken at 1440x900 and 390x844, with reduced motion, CSS animations disabled, and after `document.fonts.ready`.
  - `--repo` shoots another checkout. `--viewport-only` skips the full-page resize (see below).
  - `compare` counts changed pixels on any channel and can write highlight images.

**Fix to DS-00 found here.** `tools/ui_inventory.py` now strips the `?v=` deploy token from `/static/...` URLs inside inline scripts before hashing.

- **Why:** the three.js import map embeds one. The token differs between two checkouts, so every page that extends `base.html` failed the diff, although nothing had changed.
- **Test:** `test_deploy_token_inside_an_inline_script_is_not_a_script_edit`. A real script edit still fails.

### Known limits

- **PostgreSQL tests** have not run on this branch; see the full-suite line.
- **D&D 5e accent:** it stays the PF2e value until the DS-24a mockup (Q4). The 5e contrast column is PF2e today, apart from its HP tiers.
