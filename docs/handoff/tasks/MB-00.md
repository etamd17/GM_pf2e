## Goal

Give Cosmere campaigns a version-pinned rules backend with real Stormlight and Mistborn profiles, server-owned character changes, and reusable GM services. Keep the current UI working while the backend contracts settle.

## Approach

- Define immutable source, rule, package, binding, advancement, effect, and adventure records in the [rules schema](../../../systems/cosmere/rules/schema.py), then compile and resolve them through the [registry](../../../systems/cosmere/rules/registry.py) and [profile boundary](../../../systems/cosmere/rules/profiles.py).
- Move character builds, advancement, live effects, actions, encounters, and migrations behind revision-checked services. The main boundaries are in [character documents](../../../systems/cosmere/documents.py), [advancement](../../../systems/cosmere/advancement.py), [actions](../../../systems/cosmere/actions.py), and [application routes](../../../app.py).
- Implement Mistborn Era 1 and Era 2 in the [build rules](../../../systems/cosmere/mistborn_build.py), [runtime model](../../../systems/cosmere/mistborn_runtime.py), [action adapter](../../../systems/cosmere/mistborn_actions.py), and private normalized rules data installed in the gitignored `cosmere_data/rules/mistborn/` directory.
- Add shared GM foundations for compendium search, party summaries, encounter staging, seeded tables, custom adversaries, narrative scenes, adventure validation, and private assets under [systems/cosmere](../../../systems/cosmere/).

- [x] Mistborn builds, Era 1/Era 2 restrictions, Allomancy, Feruchemy, action costs, durations, and mixed-world dispatch are implemented with explicit automation dispositions.

## Out of scope / do not touch

- Do not redesign the Cosmere UI in this branch. The current pages are compatibility clients; the UI workshop comes after the backend contract is stable.
- Do not commit private PDFs, extracted book prose, page images, maps, tokens, handouts, or watermarks. Keep only normalized mechanics, original summaries, citations, hashes, and private-asset metadata.
- Do not auto-upgrade pinned campaigns when a new package version appears.
