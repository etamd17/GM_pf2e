## Goal
In a campaign whose Cosmere world is **Mistborn**, every generator card produces Scadrial
content: names, NPCs, weather, coins, loot, locations, hooks, rumours and scene dressing. Card
labels fit the world too. Stormlight campaigns stay exactly as they are.

## Approach
1. **Data, per world.** Keep today's Roshar lists unchanged. Add Mistborn lists beside them, or
   move each world into its own small module (`generator_roshar.py` / `generator_scadrial.py`);
   your call. Give `generate(gtype, world='stormlight')` and every `gen_*` a `world` argument.
   An unknown world falls back to Stormlight.
2. **Mistborn content, Era 1 by default.** Era 1 (the Final Empire: Luthadel, ash, mists, skaa,
   nobles, obligators, boxings and clips) is the default. If the campaign's `rules_binding` pins
   exactly one era and it is `era-2` (Elendel Basin: guns, railways, constables, banknotes),
   use Era 2 lists for the cards where it matters (NPC roles, locations, loot, coins, hooks).
   Read the era safely: `normalize_binding` raises when the Mistborn package isn't installed,
   so wrap it and fall back to Era 1. Don't add any new campaign setting for this.
   Rough sizes (match the Roshar lists):
   - Names: a few groups (e.g. noble houses, skaa, terris, Era 2 Basin), ~80 names.
   - NPC: 18 roles, 12 traits, 10 hooks.
   - Weather (label **"Mists / Weather"**): 8 entries (ashfall, thick mists, red sun...).
   - Coins (label **"Coins"**): procedural like `gen_spheres` (boxings and clips; Era 2 notes).
   - Loot (label **"Loot & Metals"**): ~14 common (metal vials, coinshot coins, ...) plus a
     few rare finds (a bead of atium is fine as a rare; no Hemalurgic spikes as common loot).
   - Location, Plot Hook, Rumour, Scene Dressing: 10-12 each.
3. **Writing rule (important, public repo).** Write every entry yourself, as short original
   lines. Canon place and people names are fine (the Roshar lists already use them), but no
   sentences or descriptions copied or closely paraphrased from the books or the Mistborn RPG.
   **Do not read or copy from** `cosmere_data/`, `data/cosmere/rules/`, or
   `systems/cosmere/content/handbook-*.json` (book-derived, and the first two are gitignored).
   `systems/cosmere/lore.py` (the 16 metals, one line each) is in the repo already and fine to
   reuse.
4. **Route and template.** The page route passes `_cosmere_world()` into
   `_gen.generate(...)` and into the card labels. The reroll route does the same, reading the
   world on the server. Don't trust a world sent by the client. "Roll 5" still makes separate
   POSTs, which is fine. Card keys (`name`, `npc`, `weather`, `spheres`, ...) **stay the same** in
   both worlds, so pins, Back history and the JS keep working. Only the labels and content change.
   The `name` card's culture picker (if any) shows the Scadrial groups under Mistborn.
5. **Docstrings:** update `generator.py`'s module docstring (it says Rosharan only).

### Required fix

   Uppercase only the first character (`s[:1].upper() + s[1:]`) in `generator_scadrial.gen_loot`,
   and add a test that every Scadrial loot line keeps its original casing after the first letter.
   Leave the Stormlight function alone (out of scope; it has the same pattern).
