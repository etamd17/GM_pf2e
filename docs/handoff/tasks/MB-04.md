# MB-04: Mistborn money (boxings and clips)

<!-- ============ BRIEF (written before work starts) ============ -->

- **Owner:** codex (standard model, high effort: this touches saved character data). Claude
  reviews the diff and checks both skins in the browser.
- **Branch:** `codex/mb-04-mistborn-money` from current `origin/main`.
- **Depends on:** none. UI-05 (gem values, v2 wallets) and MB-03 (generators) are merged.
- **User decision (2026-10-26):** one currency, **boxings and clips**, with **one fixed exchange
  rate, the same in both eras**. Era 2 banknotes are boxings in paper form, as the generators
  already describe them (`generator_scadrial.py::gen_coins`). No separate Era 2 currency.

## Context

- Roshar money is `systems/cosmere/spheres.py`: integer values in diamond chips, a versioned
  wallet (`normalize_wallet` returns `{version: 2, spheres: {gem: {chip, mark, broam}}, items}`),
  `wallet_total` and `format_marks`. `app.py` imports them around line 12171 for the loot ledger
  (~12206), the wallet totals (~12408, ~12493) and the sheet (~15717).
- The world comes from `app.py::_cosmere_world()`, and templates get `cosmere_world` from the
  context processor. Mistborn campaigns currently show and award **Roshar spheres** in wallets
  and on the loot page.

## Goal

Under the Mistborn world, character wallets and the loot page use boxings and clips: shown,
edited, awarded and totalled in them. Stormlight is unchanged, byte for byte.

## Approach

1. **The rate.** Take boxings-to-clips from the installed Mistborn rules bundle if it states one,
   and cite where in your report. If the bundle does not state a rate, **stop and ask** in your
   handoff; do not invent one. Keep values as integers in clips, so nothing passes through
   floating point, as `spheres.py` does with chips.
2. **A separate module and wallet,** e.g. `systems/cosmere/scadrial_money.py`, with the same
   shape of API as `spheres.py`: normalize, total and format.
   - Store the money under its own key in the character's wallet (for example
     `wallet["scadrial"] = {"version": 1, "boxing": n, "clip": n}`) **beside** `spheres`, never
     inside it.
   - A sphere wallet and a Scadrial wallet never convert into each other. A Mistborn character
     with existing sphere counts keeps them untouched. Show them read-only, labelled as Roshar
     spheres, only if any exist, and report how many such characters your fixtures and the dev
     data have.
3. **Opening never rewrites a file.** That is the same rule as UI-05: a character gains the new
   key only on its next real save. Test it.
4. **The loot page:**
   - Mistborn campaigns award boxings and clips;
   - a ledger entry records which currency it used;
   - deleting an award reverses exactly what it credited, as UI-05's gem-isolated reversal does,
     and can never debit the other currency;
   - totals per character and per party are in boxings and clips.
5. **The sheet:** the wallet editor and the total under the Mistborn skin show boxings and clips,
   and live repaint keeps working (check `_pc_state_payload` / the Cosmere equivalent ships it).
6. **Systems never share mechanics:** nothing PF2e or D&D changes. The Stormlight wallet, loot
   and sheet output must be identical before and after; a regression test compares them.

## Accept

- Tests for:
  - the integer value math, and normalization of missing, partial and bad input;
  - open-without-rewrite;
  - award, delete and exact reversal in both worlds, including that a Mistborn award can never
    touch spheres and a Stormlight award never touches boxings;
  - the loot totals;
  - the sheet wallet round-trip;
  - Stormlight unchanged.
- All templates parse; the full Cosmere suite passes.
- Report the rate and its source, the files touched, and real test numbers.
